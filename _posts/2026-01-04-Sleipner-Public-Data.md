### Sleipner CCS Public Data

<div style="text-align: justify"> 

  The Sleipner CCS project is renowned not just for its operational success, but for its role as a "global laboratory," made possible by Equinor’s decision to release extensive monitoring datasets to the public. This
  openness has allowed researchers worldwide to validate flow models and develop new monitoring techniques. The data can be found <a href="https://co2datashare.org/dataset?tags=sleipner">here</a>.


<dl>

  <dt>Sleipner 4D Seismic Dataset</dt>
  <dd>
    This is a massive collection (approximately 26 GB) of processed seismic cubes. Baseline Survey (1994) is the "before" picture of the Utsira Formation prior to injection. Multiple repeat time-lapse 
    surveys (including 1999, 2001, 2004, 2006, 2008, and 2010) that show the actual growth and migration of the CO2 plume over 14 years. All data are provided in standard SEG-Y format, allowing geophysicists to run their
    own inversions and attribute analyses.
  </dd>

  <dt>Sleipner 2019 Benchmark Model</dt>
  <dd>
    Released in 2020, this dataset was specifically designed to help reservoir engineers build more accurate simulation models. It provides the "scaffolding" needed to replicate the Sleipner environment.
    Simulation Grid is a ready-to-use grid compatible with software like ECLIPSE or Petrel. Well logs from two key wells (15/9-A-16 and 15/9-13), depth surfaces, and velocity maps are available.Plume boundaries
    interpreted outlines of the CO2 accumulations in different internal sandstone layers serve as the "ground truth" for matching simulation results.
  </dd>

  
  
</dl>

</div>
